1.General Information

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled by the FluffPage mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the “App”).

The App is developed and published by an individual developer (hereinafter referred to as the “Developer”).

FluffPage is a local EPUB and PDF reader designed with user privacy in mind. The App operates offline and does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Privacy contact:
Email: calmbytecontact@gmail.com

2.Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share users’ personal data with the Developer.

In particular, the App does not collect:

  • Users’ names or email addresses.
  • Location data.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Browsing or activity history transmitted to external servers.
  • Analytics data about App usage.

The App does not use user tracking or profiling mechanisms.

3.Locally Stored Data

To provide its core functionality, the App may store the following information directly on the user’s device:

  • Reading progress – information about the last reading position in a book.
  • App settings – preferences related to the appearance and behavior of the reader.
  • Recently opened books – a local history of previously opened documents.
  • Notes and text highlights – content created by users while reading.
  • Book thumbnails and covers – locally stored images used by the reader.

This information is stored locally and is not transmitted to the Developer or external services.

The Developer does not have access to the contents of books, notes, or other information stored on the user’s device.

4.File Access

The App may access EPUB and PDF documents selected by the user in order to open and display them.

File access is provided through the permissions and file selection mechanisms available in the Android operating system.

The contents of opened documents are processed locally on the user’s device and are not transmitted to external servers.

5.Sharing Data with Third Parties

The App does not share users’ data with third parties.

The App does not use advertising services, external analytics tools, or tracking mechanisms, unless such functionality is introduced in future versions.

6.Data Security

App data is stored locally in the device’s memory, using the security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system.

The Developer does not operate an external user database or a server storing information about activity within the App.

Users are also responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and creating backups of important data.

7.Data Retention and Deletion

Local data is retained on the user’s device for as long as it is needed to support the App’s functionality or until it is deleted by the user.

Users can delete locally stored information using the data deletion features available within the App, where provided, or by clearing the App’s data through Android system settings or uninstalling the App.

The Developer does not maintain copies of this data on external servers.

Please note that Android system backups may include certain App data if this feature is enabled on the device. Such backups are managed through the user’s device settings and backup services.

8.Children and Minors

The App does not require users to provide personal information or create an account. It does not collect personal data from users, including children.

9.Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy, for example, when App functionality or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version of this document will be made available at the same public URL.

If new features affecting data processing are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
